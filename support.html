Common questions

Do I need an account or internet connection?
No. All content is built in and works offline.

Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. If you delete the app, your saved topics and course progress are removed too.

How do I reset my course progress?
Delete and reinstall the app.

Is this a replacement for learning from scholars?
No. Dawah Companion is a study and conversation aid. For religious rulings, please consult qualified scholars.
