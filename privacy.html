Privacy Policy

Dawah Companion · Last updated 29 September 2026

Dawah Companion does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Your saved topics, recently viewed topics, course and quiz progress, and your light/dark appearance setting are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Android device backup) may include them, under your account with Apple or Google.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No network requests: all content is built into the app and works offline

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the text you share goes to the app you choose (Messages, Mail and so on) under that app's own privacy policy. Dawah Companion does not see it.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
